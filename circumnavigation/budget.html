Health insurance is the single largest non-haul cost
Medicare does not cover travel abroad. Combined coverage for a 65-year-old (GeoBlue / Cigna Global Platinum) and a 56-year-old international plan plus a MEDEVAC evacuation rider runs roughly $1,550 per month (~$18,600/yr).
